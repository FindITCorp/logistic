## How to Use This List

For each forwarder, the primary goal is to **get a tiered rate table**:
- 1 m³, 3 m³, 6 m³, 10 m³, 20 m³ — same route, same week
- Ask: "If I guarantee X m³/month minimum, what rate can you lock in?"

FINDIT's reference price is $100/m³ DDP. Any forwarder that can offer $80-90/m³ on 6-10 m³ pools = viable margin.
